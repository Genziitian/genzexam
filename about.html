About us

Practice that feels like the real exam.

Quiz LAB is where IITM BS students practise past papers the way the exam actually feels: a real timer, a question palette and worked solutions the moment you submit. It is built and run by GenZ IITian, a team of students who know the journey because we are on it too.

What is Quiz LAB

A practice room for every IITM BS paper.

Weekly assignments, Quiz 1, Quiz 2, End Term papers and mocks, organised by course and level. Sit a paper on the clock in exam mode, or learn as you go in practice mode, and keep every attempt in My Papers.

Who we serve

Students of the IIT Madras BS degree.

From the first Qualifier week to the Degree level, across Data Science, Electronic Systems and the other BS programmes. Whether you are revising the night before a quiz or planning a whole term, Quiz LAB is made for you.

Who runs it

Made by GenZ IITian.

GenZ IITian is a student-led community helping IITM BS learners study smarter. Alongside Quiz LAB, we share free lectures and study material, and we build tools we wished we had when we started. Quiz LAB is one part of that mission.
