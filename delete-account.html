Request Account & Data Deletion

To request account and data deletion, sign in to the QUIZ LAB mobile app and open More → Delete Account & Data. Choose a reason and submit the request for manager review.

Your account remains active while the request is reviewed. Once the request is approved and processed, your profile and associated learning records will be removed.

If you cannot access the app, email admin@genziitian.org from your registered address and include your reason.
