Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026
Google Play Store Compliance Notice: QUIZ LAB adheres to the Google Play Developer Program Policies regarding User Data. We provide transparent collection disclosures, encryption in transit and at rest, and self-serve account and data deletion mechanisms.
01

Information We Collect

To provide personalized exam preparation and learning analytics, QUIZ LAB collects:

  • Identity Data: Your name and email address provided during Google Sign-In or account registration.
  • Academic & Practice Data: Quiz attempt answers, question completion times, score breakdowns, streak history, and XP achievements.
  • Device & Network Data: IP address and client user-agent logged strictly for security monitoring and session integrity.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and grant instant access to your purchased courses and practice papers.
  • To calculate accurate score reports, auto-score evaluations, and global leaderboard rankings.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent unauthorized account sharing, and defend against malicious attacks.
03

Data Security & Storage

Your authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using hardware-backed Android Keystore with EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256-GCM). All network communications between your mobile device and our servers (https://labapi.genziitian.in) are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.

04

Third-Party Services & Payment Data

Payment transactions are processed directly by certified third-party payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe, or Cashfree). QUIZ LAB does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, or UPI PINs on our servers.

05

User Rights & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data Deletion requirements, all users have the right to permanently delete their account and associated records at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the QUIZ LAB mobile app, navigate to the More tab, and select Delete Account & Data.
  • Web Portal Deletion: Visit our dedicated web deletion request form at https://lab.genziitian.in/delete-account.html to request immediate erasure without needing the app installed.

Upon deletion, all personal data, attempts, scores, and access keys are irreversibly purged from our active databases.

06

Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact our Data Protection Team at privacy@genziitian.in or support@genziitian.in.
