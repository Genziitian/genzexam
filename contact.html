FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Most questions are answered here. If yours isn't, we're one message away.

01How quickly will I hear back?

We read every message ourselves and reply as soon as we can. WhatsApp is usually the fastest way to reach us; email is best when you need to share screenshots or order details.

02I paid but my paper is not in My Papers. What should I do?

Payments can take a few moments to confirm. Refresh My Papers first. If it is still missing, send us your registered email and the payment ID from your receipt, and we will sort it out.

03How do I ask for a refund?

Read our Refund & Cancellation policy first, then email us from your registered address with the paper name and payment ID.

04I found a wrong answer or a typo in a paper.

Thank you, this helps everyone. Send us the paper name, the question number and what looks wrong. A screenshot makes it quicker to fix.

05I can't sign in to my account.

Try Forgot password on the sign-in page, or Continue with Google if you signed up that way. If you are still stuck, write to us from the email you registered with.

06How do I delete my account and data?

You can request it from the Delete Account & Data page or from your profile. If anything is unclear, message us and we will help.

07Is Quiz LAB part of IIT Madras?

No. Quiz LAB is built by GenZ IITian for IITM BS students and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by IIT Madras.
